Goals & Philosophy

目標と理念

where we're headed, and what we believe

Our goals 目標

What we're working toward

Short-term goals for this semester, and long-term goals for after we graduate.

This semester

  • Finish what we startPublish this e-Portfolio with every member's reflections complete before the final presentation.
  • Learn one new tool eachUse it in a real output, not just a tutorial, and write down what it taught us.
  • Stay safe onlineTurn on two-factor login, use strong passwords, and think twice before posting.

After college

  • Show, don't just tellBuild a portfolio of real work that proves what we can do, not only what we studied.
  • Keep learning on our ownUse free courses, documentation, and online communities to keep our skills current.
  • Give backUse technology to help our families and communities, not only ourselves.

Our philosophy

Dear reader,

We believe technology is a tool, and a tool is only as good as the hands and intentions behind it. A phone can spread rumors, or it can keep a family close across an ocean. A spreadsheet can hide mistakes, or it can make a budget honest.

So we try to learn with curiosity and use what we learn with care. We credit the work of others, we protect the privacy of the people around us, and we choose tools that make our work clearer, not just flashier.

Growth, like a meadow, is slow and shared. Each of us planted something different this semester, and this scrapbook is where we press those leaves so we remember what we learned.

With care,

My goals, growth, and nursing journey

The required sections of my student profile, written in my own words.

01

Goals & Philosophy

Section 01

My goal is to become a competent and compassionate Registered Nurse, build a successful and stable life, provide for my loved ones, and eventually have my own dream home and family. I also want to keep learning, exploring new experiences, and improving myself every day. I believe that life is not a race and that everyone has their own pace, so I focus on becoming better than the person I was yesterday. For me, success isn't just about achieving my dreams; it is also about enjoying the journey, appreciating the people around me, and helping others achieve their dreams, too.

02

Daily Communication

Section 02

I mainly use Instagram and Facebook to communicate and stay connected with friends and family. I'm more active on Instagram, where I usually watch Reels, explore interesting content, and keep myself entertained. I also use Facebook to check updates, share posts, and communicate with others.

03

Hobbies & Talents

Section 03

Hobbies: I enjoy running, gaming, riding, spending quality time with my girlfriend, and satisfying my curiosity by learning and experiencing things firsthand. I love activities that challenge me, keep me active, and give me something new to discover.

Talents: I'm good at adapting to different situations and challenging myself; these are some of my talents. I'm also good at staying determined, especially when working toward something I really want to achieve. I may not be the best at everything, but I'm always willing to learn and improve.

04

Professional Development: Why I Want to Become a Nurse

Section 04

I want to become a nurse because I want to build a successful life while doing something meaningful for others. I love the idea of being able to help people during their most difficult moments and make even a small difference in their lives. Of course, I also have personal dreams. I want to provide for my loved ones, build my dream home, and create a happy future with my girlfriend. But I believe success becomes more meaningful when you achieve it through something that benefits others, too. For me, nursing is a way to combine my dreams with my desire to help people.

05

Professional Development: Experiences

Section 05

My experience as a nursing student has taught me the importance of patience, empathy, compassion, and teamwork in providing proper care. I particularly enjoy conducting health teachings, especially with children, because even with my limited knowledge, I can share what I've learned and help others take better care of their health. I also enjoy learning nursing procedures and improving my clinical skills. Despite the sleepless nights, overwhelming moments, and difficult lessons, I see everything as part of my growth. I may still have a lot to learn, but I always strive to become better than I was yesterday.

06

Professional Development: Where I See Myself 5 Years From Now

Section 06

Five years from now, I see myself as a Registered Nurse with enough experience, knowledge, and confidence to provide quality care to my patients. I hope to be working in a good healthcare institution where I can continue learning, improving my skills, and exploring more opportunities in the nursing profession. I also dream of becoming financially stable, helping my family, traveling to different places, and slowly building the home I've always wanted. I hope that my girlfriend, my teammates, and I will all become successful Registered Nurses. As for my girlfriend and me, I hope we will continue supporting and motivating each other as we work toward our shared dreams of building our own home and starting a happy family. Most importantly, I hope I never lose my curiosity, passion for learning, and willingness to help others. I don't expect to have everything figured out by then; I just want to make the most of every opportunity, enjoy the journey, and never give up on the life I'm working hard to achieve. At the end of the day, I want to live a life worth remembering, not just because of what I've accomplished, but also because of the people I've helped and the people who grew alongside me.

07

Data Privacy & Ethics

Section 07

I affirm that the materials presented in this e-portfolio are shared at my own discretion for academic purposes. I understand the importance of protecting personal information, respecting the privacy and confidentiality of others, and using digital platforms responsibly. I also acknowledge the importance of respecting copyright, avoiding plagiarism, and giving proper credit to original sources. As a nursing student, I recognize that confidentiality is essential in healthcare, and I commit to practicing it both online and in my future profession.

08

Testimony as a 2nd-Year Nursing Student

Section 08

Being a second-year nursing student has been a journey of challenges, learning, and self-discovery. There have been sleepless nights, overwhelming moments, and difficult lessons, but I choose to embrace them as part of becoming the person and nurse I dream of being. I may not know everything yet, but I'm willing to learn, accept corrections, and improve every day. I believe that every experience, no matter how small, brings me one step closer to my goals. For now, I'll just go with the flow, enjoy the process, and keep becoming better than I was yesterday.

08

Testimony as a 2nd-Year Nursing Student

Section 08

Being in a field where I only like interacting with people and learning about brains, it's hard. I need constant motivation and a strong will to study, go to school, and report for duty. I had constant regrets about not pursuing computer science, but now I'm making it one of my hobbies, alongside learning a new language. There were a lot of times when I felt unhappy and alone. 01

Goals & Philosophy

Section 01

My philosophy as a nursing student is that nursing goes beyond treating illnesses; it is about caring for people with compassion, dignity, and understanding. I believe that every patient has a unique story, and behind every condition is a person who deserves to be heard, respected, and cared for. I strive to become a nurse who not only provides competent care but also brings comfort, hope, and reassurance to those in need. I believe that nursing is more than a profession; it is a commitment to serve, understand, and care for others. It is built on the belief that every individual deserves compassion, respect, dignity, and quality care, regardless of their circumstances.

My goals as a nursing student are to develop discipline, accountability, patience, and resilience in facing the challenges of the nursing profession, to embrace every clinical experience as an opportunity for personal and professional growth, and lastly, to become a nurse who inspires trust, offers comfort, advocates for patients, and contributes positively to the community.

02

Daily Communication

Section 02

Technology plays a very important role in our daily lives now. We connect with our family, friends, and other people through the use of devices. Not only that, but we also use technology in our education now. I use technology to message not only my family and friends but also to connect with my clinical instructors and blockmates. I use it to listen to music, watch movies or videos, and also post on my stories for memories and archives.

03

Hobbies & Talents

Section 03

Hobbies: My hobby is calligraphy because I love how I put creativity and much more life into just words or just letters. I love putting color and making it unique that also connects with what the text says, and it's much more interesting to read and look at. Ever since I was in elementary school, I have really loved writing journals and pouring whatever is in my head through pen and paper. I joined contests such as essay writing before, and I also won as first placer when I was in senior high school. I also love playing volleyball and badminton. Before, I only played badminton and joined interschool competitions in previous years, but now volleyball has also become one of the things I love to play. On days when I don't do much and have free time, I take my time singing and recording music in BandLab, playing Roblox, and going on spontaneous dates with friends.

Talents: My talents are singing and dancing, but mostly I really lean toward singing. Ever since I was a kid, my mom has encouraged me to join pageants and glee clubs. In high school, I was also part of the glee clubs, and we had a school concert. One of my talent exhibitions is singing, and I really looked up to Sarah Geronimo and Hannah Montana when I was a little kid. I admire them for singing and showing their talents. In high school, I always joined cultural performances such as Chinese and Korean dances. I really love dancing too, and when I was in high school, I was one of the students who would teach steps for our performances, whether in school or outside malls.

04

Professional Development: Why I Want to Become a Nurse

Section 04

When I was a kid, I wasn't really leaning toward becoming a nurse. I wanted to become a flight attendant ever since, but because of practicality, I went for nursing because they say that even technology couldn't really replace nursing. As I became a nursing student and got exposed to clinical experiences, I realized that nursing is so much more than being practical and professional. I started seeing it as more than that, and even if it's hard, I realized what I truly loved the most, which is to care, be compassionate, and give kindness to people. I aspire to become a nurse who makes a difference, even through the smallest acts of kindness. Someone who listens without judgment, cares without discrimination, and provides comfort even when a cure is beyond reach. Above all, I believe that nursing is a continuous journey of learning, service, and humanity.

Professional Development: Where I See Myself 5 Years From Now

Section 06

Five years from now, I can see myself graduating with a Bachelor of Science in Nursing, and by that time, I will already be a board passer. Hopefully, I pray that I become one of the top notchers in the board exam. As soon as I pass the board exam as a PHRN, I want to get my experience as a nurse in government hospitals such as BMC in Naga or BRGHGMC in Cabusao. When I already get my experience here in the Philippines, I want to take the NCLEX exam and fly abroad to fulfill my dream as a USRN. That's truly the reason why I want to become a nurse: to fly abroad and live my dreams and life there with the people I love the most while I work myself hard for them. I have wanted to travel the world and achieve things I wanted for myself ever since I was a kid, before having my own kids. I don't really pressure myself to follow this timeline for my success, but I pray to God that I become successful enough to give back all the sacrifices my parents did for me. 08

Testimony as a 2nd-Year Nursing Student

Section 08

My experience in nursing school is not always easy and goes with the flow. You need to exert yourself beyond your limits: even when you're tired, drained, going through heartbreaks, and overwhelmed, you still have to show up to RLEs, skills labs, and most especially hospital duties; study all night for quizzes and exams; and do your lecture notes. In hospital duties, I've seen different cases of patients and their significant others. You have to be kind, compassionate, caring, and understanding with those people. I always remind myself that even if I'm tired, my patients are experiencing much worse things. In those experiences, I will always try my best and see the good in those moments. It is such a privilege to experience nursing and witness life and death. Way before I started nursing, I told myself to serve and care for people even if they are strangers. How ironic it is that I care so much for people, and yet when you become a nurse, you forget to take care of yourself. You will exert everything, and I think that's the goodness of it too. Even though you're tired, you learn how to be brave and see much more in life because of the experiences of your patients. Looking at a father beside his child on the hospital bed, praying that everything will be okay, and not only that but also remembering their hospital bills. Seeing a mother being worried and anxious when I get her baby's vital signs, crying and scared. Witnessing a dead body come across us, who just died while their family follows the staff nurses carrying his/her body to the morgue. Watching a mother bring a smile to her face when she heard her baby's heartbeat in the Doppler. Those experiences always stand in my heart, and I will carry these to remember that nursing is much more. I know that there is still more to see and learn, and I am ready to face it, even when it's hard.
